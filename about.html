Our story

A new chapter,
the same philosophy

Midas Clinic Inspire Resort continues the practice formerly known as Beauty Line Plastic Surgery (선이고은 성형외과) — an honest clinic built on trustworthy treatment. With a new name and a new home inside Inspire Entertainment Resort, our approach stays the same.

We do not design faces for trends. We study each person's structure and character, and change only what needs to change — so the result looks like you, only more refined.
