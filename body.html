Overview

Body

Body procedures are planned with the director, a member of the American Society of Lipo-Suction Surgery and the American Society of Hair Restoration Surgery, who trained in breast surgery and reconstruction in the U.S. and Japan. The focus is on natural lines rather than volume alone.

Procedures

What we offer

01

Liposuction

Refines fat layers area by area, with the overall body line in mind.

02

Abdominoplasty

Tightens loose abdominal skin and fascia, for example after pregnancy or weight loss.

03

Breast Lift

Restores the position and shape of sagging breasts.

04

Stem Cell Breast Augmentation

Breast augmentation with your own fat, grafted together with adipose-derived stem cells — natural volume and feel without implants.

05

Stem Cell Breast Reconstruction

Restores breast volume and shape after surgery, or corrects asymmetry and deformity, using your own fat and adipose-derived stem cells.

06

Hair Transplant

Natural hairline and density designed for your face shape.

Is it for you?

Who it may suit

  • Localised fat that does not respond to diet or exercise
  • Loose abdominal skin after pregnancy or weight loss
  • Wanting natural breast volume without implants, or reconstruction after breast surgery
  • Thinning hair or a receding hairline

FAQ

Questions

How long is the recovery?

It depends on the procedure, the method and each person's condition. We explain the expected recovery in detail during your consultation.

What kind of anesthesia is used?

The anesthesia method is decided after reviewing the procedure and your health history, and explained to you in advance.

Can I consult before visiting Korea?

Yes. Send us your photos and questions via KakaoTalk, LINE, WhatsApp or WeChat for a preliminary consultation. Final planning is done after an in-person examination.

※ Results and recovery vary by individual. Every surgery carries possible risks such as bleeding, infection, swelling, asymmetry or scarring. Please consult a specialist fully before deciding.
