Overview

Facial Lifting

Tightening the skin alone may not address deeper age-related changes. Lifting at MIDAS repositions tissue at the SMAS layer beneath the skin, with the aim of a natural and lasting result

Procedures

Procedures & Treatments

01

Facelift

Addresses overall facial laxity and deep folds at the SMAS level

02

Midface SMAS Lift

Repositions the cheeks and nasolabial area to restore midface contour

03

Subbrow Lift

Improves upper eyelid laxity through an incision placed below the brow

04

Neck SMAS Lift

Refines the jawline and addresses laxity of the neck

05

Cheek Lift

Addresses sagging of the cheek contour

06

Forehead Lift

Addresses forehead lines and brow ptosis

Indications

Who May Benefit

  • Laxity of the jawline, cheeks or neck
  • Deepening nasolabial folds
  • Limited results from non-surgical lifting
  • Seeking a refreshed appearance while preserving natural features
