Overview

Eye Surgery

The eyes shape first impressions. Rather than a fixed design, we look at how your eyelids open, the thickness of the skin and fat, and the proportions of your face to create eyes that look clear yet natural.

Procedures

What we offer

01

Double Eyelid (Non-incision / Partial / Full incision)

The method is chosen according to skin thickness, fat and the line you want.

02

Ptosis Correction

Strengthens weak eye-opening muscles for a brighter, more alert look.

03

Epicanthoplasty / Lateral Canthoplasty

Fine-tunes the horizontal length and proportion of the eyes.

04

Upper / Lower Blepharoplasty

Addresses sagging upper eyelids, under-eye fat and wrinkles.

Is it for you?

Who it may suit

  • Wanting a defined but natural double eyelid
  • Eyes that look tired or sleepy
  • Sagging eyelids or under-eye bags with age

FAQ

Questions

How long is the recovery?

It depends on the procedure, the method and each person's condition. We explain the expected recovery in detail during your consultation.

What kind of anesthesia is used?

The anesthesia method is decided after reviewing the procedure and your health history, and explained to you in advance.

Can I consult before visiting Korea?

Yes. Send us your photos and questions via KakaoTalk, LINE, WhatsApp or WeChat for a preliminary consultation. Final planning is done after an in-person examination.
