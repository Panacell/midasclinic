Overview

Rhinoplasty

The nose sits at the centre of the face and is also how we breathe. At Midas, rhinoplasty starts from the internal structure — cartilage support, septum and airway — so that the new line looks natural and stays stable over time.

Procedures

What we offer

01

Rib Cartilage Rhinoplasty

Your own rib cartilage is used to rebuild a strong framework — often chosen when septal cartilage is insufficient or for revision cases.

02

Septoplasty

Straightens a deviated septum to improve breathing, and can be combined with aesthetic rhinoplasty.

03

Revision Rhinoplasty

Corrects problems from previous surgery such as contracture, deviation or implant-related issues.

04

Male Rhinoplasty

A straighter, stronger line that suits male facial structure without looking overdone.

Is it for you?

Who it may suit

  • A low or flat nasal bridge, or an undefined tip
  • Breathing discomfort from a deviated septum
  • Dissatisfaction or problems after previous nose surgery
  • Wanting a natural result that suits the whole face

FAQ

Questions

How long is the recovery?

It depends on the procedure, the method and each person's condition. We explain the expected recovery in detail during your consultation.

What kind of anesthesia is used?

The anesthesia method is decided after reviewing the procedure and your health history, and explained to you in advance.

Can I consult before visiting Korea?

Yes. Send us your photos and questions via KakaoTalk, WhatsApp or WeChat for a preliminary consultation. Final planning is done after an in-person examination.
